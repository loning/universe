We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Subharmonic Corrections in Gravitational Wave Ringdowns: A Semi-Empirical Approach with Resonant Extensions" for consideration for publication in Classical and Quantum Gravity.

This paper presents a novel semi-empirical framework that extends standard quasi-normal mode analysis of black hole ringdowns to include subharmonic correction terms. While retaining compatibility with general relativity, our approach addresses observed anomalies in high-fidelity reconstructions of LIGO/Virgo gravitational wave signals, with a particular focus on GW150914 and GW190521 events.

The primary contributions of our work include:

1. Identification of statistically significant subharmonic structures in post-merger gravitational wave signals not fully captured by standard QNM templates
2. Development of a mathematically compact extension to QNM formalism that incorporates resonant subharmonic terms
3. Demonstration of improved late-time waveform reconstruction with our extended model, reducing reconstruction error by up to 35%
4. Exploration of potential physical interpretations linking these subharmonic signatures to fundamental spacetime structure

Our findings are particularly relevant for Classical and Quantum Gravity's readership as they bridge observational gravitational wave physics with theoretical investigations into quantum gravity and discrete spacetime models. The framework we develop offers a practical template extension for ringdown analysis while also potentially opening avenues for probing fundamental physics at the interface of classical and quantum gravity.

All authors have contributed substantially to this work and have approved the manuscript for submission. This manuscript has not been published or submitted for publication elsewhere. We confirm that there are no known conflicts of interest associated with this publication.
