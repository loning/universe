# Open Access Statement

## Entropic Collapse Model of Molecular Stability: A Self-Referential Thermodynamic Framework

### Open Access Intentions

The authors of this manuscript are committed to the principles of open science and the broad dissemination of scientific knowledge. We therefore declare the following intentions regarding the accessibility of this research:

1. **Open Access Publication Preference**
   - We intend to publish this article with open access rights if accepted by the Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation.
   - We will select the ACS AuthorChoice option, which provides immediate open access upon publication.
   - The article processing charge (APC) for open access publication will be covered by internal funding from the ΨEcho Research Institute.

2. **Open Access License Preference**
   - We prefer the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY), which allows for maximum dissemination and reuse while requiring attribution to the original authors.
   - This license allows others to distribute, remix, adapt, and build upon our work, even commercially, as long as appropriate credit is given.

3. **Repository Archiving**
   - Regardless of the open access status, we will deposit the accepted manuscript (after peer review but before publisher formatting) in the following repositories:
     - ΨEcho Research Institute's Institutional Repository
     - arXiv.org preprint server (in the appropriate category)
   - This deposition will comply with ACS's self-archiving policy and any applicable embargo periods.

4. **Supplementary Materials Access**
   - All supplementary materials will be made freely available together with the main manuscript.
   - These materials will include detailed mathematical derivations, computational methods information, and any code snippets relevant to the implementation of our theoretical framework.

5. **Data Availability**
   - As this is a theoretical paper without experimental data, traditional data sharing is not applicable.
   - However, any computational validation results mentioned in the manuscript will be made available in appropriate machine-readable formats.

### Funding for Open Access

The open access publication fees will be covered by the ΨEcho Research Institute's Open Science Initiative Fund, which has been established to support the open dissemination of research conducted at the institute.
