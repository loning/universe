# Collapse-Originated Quantum Gravity: A Prime-Resonant Unification of Time, Space, and Information

## Paper ID: PHY-NAT-007

This repository contains all files related to the manuscript "Collapse-Originated Quantum Gravity: A Prime-Resonant Unification of Time, Space, and Information" targeted for publication in Nature Physics.

## Abstract

We introduce a quantum gravity framework in which spacetime curvature arises not from geometrized dynamics, but from structural recursion embedded in collapse processes. This theory defines gravitational effects as emergent features of a recursive field undergoing collapse at prime-indexed attractor points, governed by internally consistent oscillatory laws. The formulation incorporates a unified evolution equation that generates gravitational dynamics through self-referential collapse, golden-ratio modulated breathing structures, and informational curvature couplings. The model yields predictions testable in black hole information interference and gravitational wave subharmonic structures.
