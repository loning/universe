## Historical Impact

Nikola Tesla's inventions fundamentally changed the modern world, with his AC system becoming the foundation of modern power transmission. The "War of Currents" between Tesla and Edison ended with AC's victory, establishing the technological path for global power infrastructure. His pioneering work in wireless communication paved the way for radio, radar, and modern communication technologies. Tesla held nearly 300 patents covering electric motors, transformers, generators, lighting, X-rays, and wireless technology. Though not fully recognized during his lifetime, he is now acknowledged as one of history's greatest inventors, with the Tesla unit (magnetic flux density) named in his honor.
