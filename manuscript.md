# Entropic Collapse Model of Molecular Stability: A Self-Referential Thermodynamic Framework

## Abstract

We propose a novel theoretical framework for predicting molecular stability based on entropic collapse principles. This framework unifies molecular thermodynamics, quantum decoherence, and information theory into a single formalism, offering a powerful new approach to analyze molecular stability without empirical input.

## 1. Introduction

Molecular stability has traditionally been understood through thermodynamic parameters such as free energy. Our approach reconceptualizes stability as an information-theoretic process governed by entropy collapse dynamics.

## 2. Theory

Our framework builds on three fundamental principles: information entropy minimization, self-referential processes, and quantum-classical boundary dynamics.
