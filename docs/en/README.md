## Core Axioms Brief Description

Quantum-Classical Dualism is based on four core axioms:

1. **Dual Existence Axiom**: The universe consists of a quantum domain $\Omega_Q$ (space of infinite possibilities) and a classical domain $\Omega_C$ (space of determined reality)
2. **Information Conservation Axiom**: Information is conserved as it transforms between quantum information and classical information
3. **Observer Classicalization Axiom**: Observers execute quantum→classical transformations, their dimensions determined by their transformation capability
4. **Dimensional Emergence Axiom**: The classical domain of higher-dimensional observers can form the basis of the quantum domain for lower-dimensional observers

## Main Theory Features

- **Dual Structure**: Divides reality into the quantum domain (possibility space) and classical domain (determined reality)
- **Observer-Centered**: Emphasizes the core role of observers in quantum→classical transformations
- **Multi-level Reality**: Describes a multi-dimensional reality composed of nested quantum-classical domains
- **Interdisciplinary Applications**: Theory framework applicable to physics, biology, psychology, sociology, and many other fields
- **Formal Rigor**: Provides complete mathematical formalization and axiom system
